MY COMFORT SHOWS

My Favorites

The stories that made me laugh, cry, and fall in love with their worlds. From anime to dramas and movies, here are my ultimate favorites.

Your Name

Anime

Makoto Shinkai's masterpiece is a breathtaking blend of romance, fantasy, and the concept of time. The animation is absolutely stunning, and the soundtrack by Radwimps perfectly captures the raw emotions of the film. It tells the story of two teenagers who swap bodies and form a deep connection across time and space. The plot twist and the ending always leave me in tears. It's a must-watch for anyone who believes in the power of fate and love.

Horimiya

Anime

A refreshing and realistic take on high school romance. Unlike other anime that drag out the "will they, won't they" trope, Horimiya shows the natural progression of a relationship and the importance of honesty. I love how it highlights that everyone has a different side to them outside of school—some are popular, some are quiet, but everyone has their own struggles. It's a warm, fuzzy comfort watch that feels like a hug.

Family by Choice

K-Drama

A heartwarming and deeply emotional story about found family. It beautifully portrays that family isn't always about blood—it's about the people who choose to stay by your side through thick and thin. The chemistry between the characters is amazing, and the way they navigate through life's challenges together is truly inspiring. It's a drama that will make you appreciate the people you love and remind you that home is where the heart is.

Hwayugi

K-Drama

A fun, chaotic, and magical fantasy romance that never fails to entertain. The story follows a group of demons and gods living in the modern world, and the characters are absolutely hilarious but also deeply lovable. The romance is full of twists and turns, and the blend of comedy and fantasy is perfect. It's the kind of drama that you can binge-watch in one sitting because every episode ends with a cliffhanger. It's pure fun and magic!

Our Secret Diaries

J-Drama

A cute and nostalgic Japanese drama that brings back the simple joys of youth, friendship, and first love. It's the kind of show that makes you smile and feel warm inside. The storytelling is gentle, and the characters feel like friends you've known your whole life. It reminds me of the times when life was simpler, and the biggest problem was whether your crush liked you back. It's a beautiful, wholesome, and comforting watch that I always go back to.

A Love So Beautiful

C-Drama

A classic and sweet high school romance that perfectly captures the innocence of first love and the bittersweet pain of growing up. The story follows a cheerful girl and a quiet, smart boy, and the way their relationship develops over the years is truly heartwarming. The chemistry between the leads is so natural, and the supporting characters are just as lovable. It's a drama that will make you laugh, cry, and remember your own first love. It's a timeless classic!

The Notebook

Movie

The ultimate romantic movie that has stood the test of time. It's a beautiful and heartbreaking story about enduring love, memory, and the passage of time. The chemistry between Ryan Gosling and Rachel McAdams is electric, and the film's emotional depth is truly captivating. It shows that love can overcome any obstacle, even when everything seems impossible. It's a movie that will make you cry buckets and leave you believing in true love.

The Avengers

Movie

The movie that started it all for me in the Marvel universe. It's action-packed, funny, and full of iconic moments that still give me goosebumps to this day. The first time the team assembled on screen was a cinematic milestone, and the chemistry between the heroes is what makes it so special. It's a perfect blend of action, humor, and heart. If you're a Marvel fan, this is a movie you can watch over and over again without getting tired of it.
