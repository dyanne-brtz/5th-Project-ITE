MY COMFORT SHOWS

My Favorites

The stories that made me laugh, cry, and fall in love with their worlds. From anime to dramas and movies, here are my ultimate favorites.

Your Name

Anime

Makoto Shinkai's masterpiece is a breathtaking blend of romance, fantasy, and the concept of time. The animation is absolutely stunning, and the soundtrack by Radwimps perfectly captures the raw emotions of the film. It tells the story of two teenagers who swap bodies and form a deep connection across time and space. The plot twist and the ending always leave me in tears.

Horimiya

Anime

A refreshing and realistic take on high school romance. Unlike other anime that drag out the "will they, won't they" trope, Horimiya shows the natural progression of a relationship and the importance of honesty. I love how it highlights that everyone has a different side to them outside of school. It's a warm, fuzzy comfort watch that feels like a hug.

Family by Choice

K-Drama

A heartwarming and deeply emotional story about found family. It beautifully portrays that family isn't always about blood—it's about the people who choose to stay by your side. The chemistry between the characters is amazing, and the way they navigate through life's challenges together is truly inspiring.

Hwayugi

K-Drama

A fun, chaotic, and magical fantasy romance that never fails to entertain. The story follows a group of demons and gods living in the modern world, and the characters are absolutely hilarious but also deeply lovable. The blend of comedy and fantasy is perfect. It's pure fun and magic!

Our Secret Diaries

J-Drama

A cute and nostalgic Japanese drama that brings back the simple joys of youth, friendship, and first love. It's the kind of show that makes you smile and feel warm inside. The storytelling is gentle, and the characters feel like friends you've known your whole life. It's a beautiful, comforting watch.

A Love So Beautiful

C-Drama

A classic and sweet high school romance that perfectly captures the innocence of first love and the bittersweet pain of growing up. The story follows a cheerful girl and a quiet, smart boy, and the way their relationship develops over the years is truly heartwarming. It's a timeless classic!

The Notebook

Movie

The ultimate romantic movie that has stood the test of time. It's a beautiful and heartbreaking story about enduring love, memory, and the passage of time. The chemistry between Ryan Gosling and Rachel McAdams is electric, and the film's emotional depth is truly captivating. It shows that love can overcome any obstacle.

The Avengers

Movie

The movie that started it all for me in the Marvel universe. It's action-packed, funny, and full of iconic moments that still give me goosebumps to this day. The first time the team assembled on screen was a cinematic milestone, and the chemistry between the heroes is what makes it so special.
